# PAYMENT VERIFICATION DESK CARD
## Stop. Check. Confirm.
For shop counters, payment desks and staff approving transfers.

### MONEY COMING IN: Before you release goods or complete an order
1. **Check your own trusted banking channel.** Open the business bank app or another reliable bank-approved account view yourself. Do not accept a screenshot, SMS or customer alert as proof of cleared funds.
2. **Match the details.** Check the amount and relevant payment details against the order. If pending or unclear, wait and verify with the bank.
3. **Use one agreed decision-maker.** Staff should know who is authorised to confirm payment.
4. **If not confirmed, do not release goods yet.** Explain that the order can be released after payment confirmation.

### MONEY GOING OUT: Before you pay a supplier or contractor
1. Check the request against the expected invoice/order.
2. If supplier details change, use a previously trusted telephone number to confirm; never rely on the contact in the change message.
3. Check beneficiary details on the transfer screen.
4. Follow your approval rule; higher-value payments may require a second authorised check.
5. Keep payment evidence in approved secure records.
