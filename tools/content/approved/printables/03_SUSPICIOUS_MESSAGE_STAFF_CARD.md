# SUSPICIOUS MESSAGE STAFF CARD
## Pause. Check. Report.
For business WhatsApp, SMS, email and social-media messages.

### PAUSE
Look for requests for passwords, OTPs or PINs; sudden payment urgency; secrecy; unexpected links; or familiar names making unusual demands. Familiar photos and display names do not prove identity.

### CHECK
Use a separate trusted route: call a saved number or independently open the official app. Verify the request, not only the sender; a genuine contact might have a compromised account. Never share a login code.
Suggested reply: “I will confirm this through our usual contact details before taking action.”

### REPORT
Tell your supervisor or account owner promptly. Preserve messages safely; do not forward to unrelated groups. If you clicked, shared credentials or sent money, report immediately and contact the affected provider or bank.
