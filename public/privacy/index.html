Privacy Policy

CyberSafe is a static website. It has no accounts, no forms and no analytics, so there is very little to tell you.

What we collect

Nothing. This site has no sign-up, no contact form, no newsletter and no analytics or advertising scripts. We do not set tracking cookies and we do not build a profile of you.

The security checklist

When you tick items on the security checklist, your ticks are saved in your own browser using local storage so the page remembers them on your next visit. That data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data removes it, and the page has a "Clear all ticks" button.

Fonts and hosting

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers and they will see your IP address as part of serving that file. The site is hosted on Netlify, whose servers process standard request logs. Neither is used by us for tracking.

Links out

We link to government agencies, security bodies and tools. Once you follow a link, that organisation's own privacy policy applies. We are not affiliated with any of them.

Email

If you email us, we will have your message and your address, and we will use them only to reply. Please do not send passwords, OTPs, BVN details, card numbers or customer records.

Questions

Write to hello@cybersafesmenigeria.ng. If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page.
