Spot the Scam: Five safety habits

The interactive game requires JavaScript. You can still learn from these five CyberSafe SME Nigeria guides:

  1. Check incoming payments independently: Fake Bank Alerts.
  2. Never share verification codes: WhatsApp Hijacking.
  3. Confirm supplier account changes: Business Email Compromise.
  4. Open delivery services yourself: Phishing Guide.
  5. Follow payment approval procedures: Payment Security.
