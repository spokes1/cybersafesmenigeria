Cybersecurity Organisations

Nigerian bodies with a role in cybersecurity, fraud and data protection — and what each one is actually useful for.

NITDA

National Information Technology Development Agency

Publishes Nigeria's IT standards and cybersecurity guidelines, including guidance aimed at MSMEs.

Visit site (opens in a new tab)ngCERT

Nigeria Computer Emergency Response Team

The national body for cyber incident reporting and advisories. This is where you report an incident.

Report an incident (opens in a new tab)NDPC

Nigeria Data Protection Commission

Oversees the Nigeria Data Protection Act. Relevant to any business holding customer or staff records.

Visit site (opens in a new tab)CBN

Central Bank of Nigeria

Consumer protection guidance, e-payment directives and fraud notices affecting business banking.

Visit site (opens in a new tab)EFCC

Economic and Financial Crimes Commission

Handles online fraud and financial cybercrime, including fraudulent transfers.

Visit site (opens in a new tab)NCC

Nigerian Communications Commission

Regulates mobile operators. Relevant for SIM registration disputes and telecoms complaints.

Visit site (opens in a new tab)

CyberSafe is not affiliated with any of these organisations. The links are provided because these are the official channels a Nigerian business would use.
