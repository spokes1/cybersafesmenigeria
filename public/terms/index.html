Terms of Use

What CyberSafe is for, and the limits of what you should rely on it for.

General information, not professional advice

Everything on CyberSafe is general cybersecurity awareness information for small and medium businesses. It is not a security audit, an incident response service or professional advice about your specific systems. Where the stakes are high — a live incident, a regulatory question, a significant loss — get qualified help.

The security checklist

The checklist is a self-assessment to help you see gaps. A full score does not mean your business is secure, and it is not a certification or a compliance statement.

External links and tools

We link to third-party organisations and tools because we think they are useful. We do not control them, we are not affiliated with them, we receive nothing for linking to them, and we cannot be responsible for their content, availability or terms.

Accuracy

We work to keep pages correct and current, but software menus change and threats evolve. Content is provided as-is, without warranty. If you spot something wrong, please tell us so we can correct it.
