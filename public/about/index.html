About CyberSafe SME Nigeria

Practical cybersecurity knowledge for Nigerian businesses.

Running a business involves more than serving customers and managing daily operations. It also means protecting payments, business accounts, customer information and the digital tools people rely on.

CyberSafe SME Nigeria is a free cybersecurity awareness and learning platform created to make that responsibility easier to understand.

We help small-business owners and their teams recognise common online threats, develop safer everyday habits and find practical guidance when something goes wrong.

Why CyberSafe exists

Not every business has an IT department or a cybersecurity specialist. Yet a small shop, online seller, growing company or independent professional may depend on digital payments, WhatsApp, email and connected devices every day.

CyberSafe helps bridge the knowledge gap by making cybersecurity information easier to access, understand and apply.

What you can do with CyberSafe

  • Understand common threats. Learn how phishing, fake bank alerts, account hijacking and other scams work.
  • Protect everyday business activities. Find practical steps for accounts, payments, phones, computers and important records.
  • Review your security habits. Use our free 15-question Security Self-Check to identify practices to improve or verify.
  • Use practical resources. Access printable security cards, templates and trusted external learning materials.
  • Know where to turn. Find general incident-response guidance and links to appropriate Nigerian organisations.

A resource built for learning

CyberSafe focuses on education, not selling cybersecurity products or services.

We do not monitor businesses, conduct security audits, investigate incidents or guarantee protection. Where specialist assistance is needed, we encourage businesses to seek qualified support.

Our goal is simple: help Nigerian businesses make more informed cybersecurity decisions, one practical step at a time.
