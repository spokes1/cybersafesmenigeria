Our Mission

Making cybersecurity knowledge accessible, practical and relevant to Nigerian SMEs.

Our mission is to help Nigerian small and medium-sized businesses understand digital threats, adopt safer everyday practices and make informed decisions when cybersecurity incidents occur.

We believe useful cybersecurity knowledge should not depend on the size of a business, its technical expertise or its ability to pay for specialist advice.

Why this matters

Digital tools help businesses communicate, receive payments, manage customers and deliver services. They also introduce risks that are not always easy to recognise.

A suspicious WhatsApp message, altered supplier invoice or compromised business email account can create serious difficulties for a small business.

Business owners and employees need guidance they can understand and apply without first becoming cybersecurity professionals.

How we approach cybersecurity education

  • Relevant to Nigeria. We prioritise situations that Nigerian businesses can recognise, while drawing on reliable local and international guidance.
  • Practical, not complicated. We explain what can go wrong, what to look out for and which reasonable steps people can take.
  • Clear and responsible. We avoid unsupported statistics, exaggerated claims, unnecessary fear and promises of guaranteed protection.
  • Free to access. Visitors can read our educational content and use our available resources without creating an account or paying a subscription.
  • Open to correction. Cybersecurity guidance evolves. We aim to review our content, verify important sources and correct information when necessary.

What we hope to achieve

We want CyberSafe to become a useful starting point for Nigerian businesses looking to strengthen their cybersecurity awareness.

That means helping more business owners recognise suspicious requests, verify payments, protect important accounts, safeguard business information and know the appropriate first actions when something goes wrong.

We will continue improving our guidance and learning resources as the platform develops.

Our commitment

CyberSafe SME Nigeria is an independent educational initiative, not a cybersecurity consultancy, incident-response provider or certification body.

We aim to provide trustworthy starting guidance while recognising that some problems require qualified technical, financial or legal assistance.

Safer business practices begin with better understanding and consistent action.
