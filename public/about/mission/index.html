Our Mission

To make cybersecurity understandable and actionable for Nigerian businesses that do not have anyone whose job it is.

A shop in Ikeja, a logistics firm in Aba and a two-person agency in Port Harcourt all face the same fraud as a bank: fake credit alerts, hijacked WhatsApp accounts, invoices with altered account numbers, phishing aimed at the one mailbox that resets everything else. What they do not have is a security team, a training budget, or time to read a forty-page framework.

CyberSafe exists to translate. We take the security practices that genuinely reduce risk, strip out the vocabulary that gets in the way, and write them as steps a business owner can act on the same day.

How we decide what to publish

  • Local first. If a threat is common against Nigerian businesses, it gets covered before something that is merely interesting.
  • Actionable over complete. Five steps someone will actually take beat forty they will not.
  • Plain language before terminology. Where a technical term is unavoidable, we explain it in the same sentence.
  • No invented numbers. We do not publish statistics, percentages or claims we cannot support. If we do not know, we leave it out.
  • Free and open. No paywall, no account, no email capture before you can read something.

What we will not do

We will not present ourselves as a security vendor, recommend a product because someone paid us, or use fear to push a business into a purchase. Where a business needs professional help, such as a real audit, incident response or compliance work, the honest answer is to say so, and we will.

How this is built

CyberSafe is developed in phases. The published pages cover threats, account and payment security, incident response, a security self-check and a set of vetted external resources. More topics are being written, and each one is listed in the Learn index whether it is finished or not, so you can see what is coming rather than guessing.

Corrections welcome

Security guidance goes out of date, and settings menus move. If something here is wrong, unclear or no longer matches what you see on screen, tell us at hello@cybersafesmenigeria.ng and we will fix it.
