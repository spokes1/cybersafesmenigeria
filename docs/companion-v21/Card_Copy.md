# Kenny bubble copy

This catalogue contains the exact strings in `card-copy.js`. Only the body text is displayed in a bubble. Titles below are editorial/accessibility labels; they are not extra visual headings.

The invitation shows its message, **Play now** and **×**. The default cyber tips show their message and **×**. Optional encouragement text below is for developer/game-feedback use only.

## Invitation

Editorial/accessibility label: Can you spot the scam?

**Message:** Try a quick scam-spotting challenge with Kenny.

Action: **Play now** · Close: **×**

## Cyber safety tips

### 1. Check new bank details

Call your usual supplier on a trusted number before paying a new bank account.

### 2. Keep your OTP private

Never share your OTP, PIN or recovery code, even with someone claiming to be your bank.

### 3. Open the official app

Open the official app or type the web address instead of following an unexpected link.

### 4. Add another lock

Turn on multi-factor authentication for business email and accounts that support it.

### 5. Verify urgent requests

Confirm urgent payment requests through a separate, trusted channel before sending money.

### 6. Confirm the credit

Check the credit in your own bank app. Payment screenshots and messages can be faked.

### 7. Use different passwords

Use a unique strong password for each important account. A password manager can help.

### 8. Check the sender

Check the full email address. A familiar name or logo does not prove who sent it.

### 9. Keep devices updated

Install security updates for your phone, browser and work apps when available.

### 10. Report suspicious payments

Contact your bank through official support quickly. Keep messages and payment details.

## Optional encouragement — developer/game-feedback copy

### 1. Keep that checking habit

A short pause and an independent check can help protect your business.

### 2. You can take your time

Pressure is a reason to pause. Verify a request before you act.

### 3. Share what you learned

Remind your team to verify changed bank details and keep passwords and OTPs private.

### 4. Small checks matter

Keep practising. Checking the sender and confirming payments are useful everyday habits.

## Accessibility and setup strings

The close icon is ×. Its accessible name and the peeker button names come from the same content module. The setup string describes a disabled Play control if the game has not been connected; it is not an extra visitor-facing message.

| Key | Text |
| --- | --- |
| `closeLabel` | Close Kenny’s message |
| `setupHelp` | Connect Play to your game. |
| `inviteAria` | Open Kenny’s game invitation |
| `tipAria` | Ask Kenny for a cyber safety tip |

The default companion rotates only the 10 cyber tips, for both played and unplayed visitors. The four encouragements remain available to the developer or game-feedback flow; they are not included in the default visitor rotation.
