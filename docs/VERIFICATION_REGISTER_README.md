# Evidence register and deferred launch decisions

This register is a factual **evidence queue**, not proof that the assets are licenced or that old published claims have been independently verified. The 6 November 2026 owner handoff said some checks had already been completed, but it did **not** identify named items with dated proof. Consequently no item has been promoted to `OWNER_CONFIRMED`, `RIGHTS_DOCUMENTED` or `SOURCE_CHECKED` without that evidence.

The three recently approved editorial handoffs (Employee Security, Data Protection, Business Email Compromise) contain source lists described as checked; their original URLs are included with that provenance. The file does not claim that they were rechecked in this task.

**Status values:** `SOURCE_CHECKED` (original accessible and claim verified); `OWNER_CONFIRMED` (named owner attestation); `RIGHTS_DOCUMENTED` (licence/permission proven); `PENDING_EVIDENCE` (cannot yet independently verify or document rights); `DEFERRED_OWNER_DECISION` (domain/contact or approval pending). Statuses may be updated item-by-item after dated proofs are supplied.

**Cloud Lambda:** No existing cloud tracking endpoint/schema was present in the repository. No Lambda or other cloud infrastructure was created, contacted or modified. The CSV is stored in the repo as the reviewable tracking record.

**Legacy source list:** The archived `docs/archive/EDITORIAL-REPORT_legacy.md` identifies the Guardian Nigeria, TheCable, BusinessDay, Punch, Surfshark and Kaspersky sources as outstanding. Do not treat the 2026 editorial report's descriptions as proof.
